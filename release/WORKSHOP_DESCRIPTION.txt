[h1]MicroDuck — Real AI Walking & Roller Skates[/h1]
A tiny physical robot driven by Pollen Robotics' actual learned neural policies.
Walking, sitting, standing, somersaults, left/right kicks, and a separate roller
variant with four freely rotating wheels and a learned crouch cycle.

[h2]Required companion download — Windows 64-bit[/h2]
Subscribing supplies the models, controller and learned policies. You must also
install the matching native physics runtime. Workshop cannot distribute DLLs.

Runtime download: {{RUNTIME_DOWNLOAD_URL}}
Download/install guide: {{INSTALL_GUIDE_URL}}

Close GMod. Extract MicroDuck_Runtime_Windows_x64.zip and merge its garrysmod
folder into your GarrysMod installation. The final files belong directly in
garrysmod/lua/bin. Install Microsoft's current Visual C++ v14 x64 Redistributable
if needed, then start 64-bit GMod. No Python or GPU is required.

[h2]Spawn[/h2]
Sandbox → Entities → MicroDuck → Real Policy or Roller Skates.
Weapons → MicroDuck → MicroDuck Remote. Aim and left-click to drive.

[h2]Remote[/h2]
W/S walk; A/D turn; arrow keys strafe. On rollers, W pushes and S brakes.
Shift+E switches between your player viewpoint and the chase camera.
E sits/stands, or runs the roller crouch cycle. F performs a walking somersault.
Shift+left/right click triggers the corresponding walking kick.
Right-click toggles motors; R resets; Space returns movement to your player.
Stop and balance before tricks. Place a small prop near the chosen foot to kick.

[h2]Scope[/h2]
Experimental local single-player addon tested on gm_flatgrass. Windows 64-bit
native runtime required. Multiplayer and uneven terrain remain unverified.
Roller falls may require R. Individual-piece physgun pickup is disabled.
Prop coupling is approximate. There is no path-planning or visual prop aiming.
